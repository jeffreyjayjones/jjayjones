Who I am

The work is writing. The value is judgment.

I’m a communications leader and writer who has spent a career learning complicated things well enough to explain them clearly and show why they matter.

The settings have ranged from Big Four consulting and enterprise technology to digital identity, fintech, fraud prevention, cybersecurity, and financial crime. The through-line has been consistent: find and talk to the experts, understand the substance, identify the story, make it credible, and build the system that carries it into the business and the market.

Writing is still the foundation. The larger job is judgment: deciding what an organization should say, why anyone should care, who needs to hear it, and how communications can help move the business and give people something to act on.
